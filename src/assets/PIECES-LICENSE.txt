Chess piece artwork
===================

The twelve piece images (white_*.png, black_*.png) are the "Cburnett" set:
SVG chess pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett, from Wikimedia Commons. They are the
same drawings this project already used - the previous copies were simply the
45-pixel renders, which went soft once the board could be drawn larger. These
are re-rendered from the same SVGs at 330 pixels and scaled down at draw time.

  Source:  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces
  Author:  Colin M.L. Burnett (User:Cburnett)

CC BY-SA requires that the author is credited and that redistributed copies
(including modified ones) carry the same licence. Keeping this file next to the
images satisfies that.

The originals are preserved in original-45px/ in case they are ever wanted back.

To re-render at a different size, fetch:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Chess_klt45.svg?width=512
substituting the piece code: p/n/b/r/q/k, then l (light) or d (dark), then t.
Wikimedia rate-limits anonymous fetches - send a descriptive User-Agent and
leave a second or so between requests.

The board itself is no longer an image; Board::renderBoard() draws the squares
and the rank/file labels, which stay crisp at any size.
