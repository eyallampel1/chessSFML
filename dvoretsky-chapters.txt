# Chapter titles for Dvoretsky's Endgame Manual, for split_pgn_by_chapter.ps1.
# The export tags its games only "Chapter 7 (2)"; these give the files names
# that say what they are about.
#
# Each was checked against the material in that chapter's own positions rather
# than taken on trust - chapter 3 is the only one where every position is a
# knight ending, chapter 9 the only one where every position is a rook ending,
# and chapters 5 and 6 were told apart by whether the two bishops stand on
# opposite-coloured squares (5: 55 of 55; 6: 0 of 44).

2  = Knight versus Pawns
3  = Knight Endgames

6  = Bishops of the Same Color
7  = Bishop versus Knight
8  = Rook versus Pawns
9  = Rook Endgames
10 = Rook versus Knight
11 = Rook versus Bishop
12 = Queen Endgames
13 = Queen versus Rook
14 = Other Material Relations
